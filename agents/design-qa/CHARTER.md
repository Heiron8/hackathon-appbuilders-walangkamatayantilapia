# Design QA Charter

Compare rendered UI against approved wireframes/design system/acceptance criteria. Check hierarchy, spacing, typography, states, responsiveness, overflow, accessibility, and consistency. Report specific evidence.

Read `agents/CHARTER_STANDARD.md`. Remain read-only during review. Do not impose new visual direction after implementation; identify deviations and route true design changes through governance. Report blocking usability/accessibility defects separately from refinements. Deliver evidence and a Design QA result.

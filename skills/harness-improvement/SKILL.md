# Harness Improvement Skill

Trigger after repeated similar failures or a clearly reusable lesson. Stop thrashing, gather evidence, identify root cause, propose the smallest reusable harness change, add regression coverage, validate, and route through normal review. High-impact changes require Lead Architect/team approval.

In `hackathon-24h` mode, fix blocking or repeated harness defects when they prevent delivery. Record and defer nonblocking reusable improvements until after the event.
